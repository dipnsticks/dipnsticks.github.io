Policies

Simple rules so everyone gets a fair, fast fix.

  1. Please Check Your Order

    Please check your items before leaving the counter so we can fix anything right away.

  2. Quality Complaints Before Consumption

    If something isn’t right, tell us before you eat it and we’ll gladly look into it.

  3. Product Fully Consumed

    Once a product has been fully consumed, we’re unable to assess a quality concern.

  4. Partially Consumed Products

    Please keep the product and packaging with you so our team can review the concern fairly.

  5. No Refund After Full Consumption

    Refunds are not available once an item has been fully eaten.

  6. Immediate Reporting

    Please report any issue as soon as you notice it so we can help quickly.

  7. Preparation Time

    Everything is cooked fresh, so a short wait is normal. Busy hours may take a little longer.

  8. Allergies & Dietary Requirements

    Tell our team about any allergy or dietary need before ordering so we can advise you.

  9. Order & Payment

    Please check your order and total before paying.

  10. Fair Resolution

    Every concern is reviewed respectfully, and we aim for a fair outcome for everyone.
